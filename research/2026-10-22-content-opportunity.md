# AION 2 content opportunity research — 2026-10-22

## Executive decision

The demand is real and still moving, but the most useful near-term expansion is not “publish every keyword.” The site should win on fast, carefully sourced explanations of new Global patches while keeping weak or region-mismatched build claims out of indexed pages.

This pass therefore shipped four concrete improvements locally:

1. A current Ariel Daevanion guide.
2. A current Krao Cave and Draupnir Hard Mode guide.
3. A Halloween Candy Key and rewards guide.
4. A substantially expanded server-transfer guide and Oct 21 update log.

Class builds remain the largest evergreen cluster, but current SERPs are crowded and the existing pages do not yet contain enough Global evidence to deserve indexing. Gladiator and Cleric are the first validation targets because they combine demand with clear role-specific intent.

## Search intent and competitive shape

### Patch systems: best current editorial opening

Ariel/Hard Mode is unusually attractive because the query is new, official facts exist, and the result set is still dominated by fresh discussions and videos. A written page can answer entry requirements, morph ratios, limits and unknowns faster than a long video, while linking to the official patch.

Candy Keys are similarly time-sensitive and task-oriented. The answer must immediately explain which color yields what, how keys are made, the weekly limit and item expiry. A generic Halloween recap would not satisfy that intent.

### Builds: valuable but crowded

Build SERPs contain Aion2.gg, Aion2Data, QuestLog-style planners, databases and many YouTube guides. A page that only lists two tooltips cannot compete and may mislead players. The differentiator must be versioned Global evidence, clear PvE/PvP and healing/DPS splits, timestamped video citations, and explicit conflicts.

### Timers and maps: product intent, not article intent

Spacetime Rift timers and Hidden Cube maps are dominated by purpose-built tools. An SEO article would be a weak substitute. These should only be pursued if the site can maintain the schedule/coordinates and provide a usable interactive or structured lookup.

### Thin item lookups: consolidate

Queries such as Sweet Balaur Berry may be hot but do not automatically justify standalone pages. Unless the item needs a meaningful acquisition route, choice, calculation or troubleshooting flow, it belongs in a maintained item index or a broader progression guide.

## Competitor lessons

| Competitor pattern | What users get | Gap this site can exploit | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Build planner/database | Structured skills and saved builds | Explain why, patch/region, conflicts and failure modes | Replicating a planner is expensive to maintain |
| Fast news/guide publisher | Broad patch coverage | Use primary sources and mark unknowns instead of paraphrasing rumors | Speed without verification creates stale facts |
| YouTube creator | Demonstration and player experience | Create scan-friendly, timestamped summaries | Advice may be KR/TW or an older client |
| Live timer/map tool | Immediate utility | Link or build the actual utility | Static prose does not satisfy live-tool intent |
| Official NC support/news | Authoritative facts | Translate scattered notices into task-oriented steps | Official notes rarely include a full build or route |

## Business and operating implications

- Acquisition: organic search around current guides, builds and live-operation questions; the evidence supports demand but not attainable rankings.
- Payer/paywall: this is a free fan wiki. No evidence currently supports a subscription or paywall. Future monetization would more plausibly be ads or affiliate-safe placements, subject to policy and traffic.
- Ongoing cost: patch monitoring, rechecking official notices, validating client/version differences, and maintaining any future timer/map data. This human freshness burden is the main recurring cost.
- Renewal/retention: repeat visits are most plausible for current builds, patch notes, server schedules, timers and maps—not one-off launch articles.
- Measurement: after deployment, track impressions and clicks by page/query in Search Console. Third-party volume should not be used as a success metric.

## Evidence boundaries

- Google Trends values are relative indices.
- Google suggestions are discovery evidence, not volume.
- Similarweb numbers are estimates from an older export.
- Video advice is not official and must be tagged by region/version.
- No page should claim a drop rate, best build, board route or Combat Power threshold unless a current source actually provides it.
